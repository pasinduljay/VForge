## 1. Executive Summary & Honest DR Scope Statement

During the Final Independent Verification and Close-Out on `chore/final-release-verification`, [`scripts/dr/backup_and_verify.ps1`](../scripts/dr/backup_and_verify.ps1) and [`docs/runbooks/BACKUP_AND_DR.md`](runbooks/BACKUP_AND_DR.md) were expanded to cover all 5 persistent Docker volumes (`vforge_postgres_data`, `vforge_rabbitmq_data`, `vforge_valkey_data`, `vforge_artifacts`, `vforge_grype_db`) without modifying or deleting any live persistent volume.

> **Important Engineering Honesty Note:** Full-platform disaster recovery is **not** claimed from PostgreSQL alone. Below is the exact per-service breakdown of what has been **independently restored in isolated containers**, what is **verified via provenance manifest + reproducible offline re-hydration**, and what remains an **external production infrastructure prerequisite**.
